# Star Client v0.15

> Welcome to Star Client **v0.15**! A high-performance, modular Fabric client for Minecraft 26.2 featuring glassmorphic UI, custom HUD widgets, and game enhancements.

---

## What's New

### New Modules, Friends System & Client Enhancements
- [NEW] **Storage Finder (`WORLD` — Hotkey: `X`)**: Automatically indexes opened Chests, Double Chests, Barrels, Shulker Boxes (including nested Shulker contents), Hoppers, and Dispensers per world/dimension — search any item, custom name, enchantment, or potion effect and highlight matching containers in 3D with pulsing boxes, item counts (`×256`), distances, optional tracers, and 1-click Waypoint pinning.
- [NEW] **World Map (`WORLD` — Hotkey: `M`)**: Fullscreen zoomable & pannable interactive World Map caching explored terrain per world/server and dimension to disk (`worldmap/`), showing live Waypoints (with 8:1 Nether sync), loaded players & friends, optional chunk grid (`G`), live cursor coordinates/biome/distance, and a right-click context menu to create/edit/delete Waypoints or copy coordinates.
- [NEW] **Gear Comparison (`UI`)**: Side-by-side glassmorphic comparison card beside item tooltips when hovering armor, weapons, or tools — highlighting upgrade verdicts (`▲ UPGRADE`, `▼ LOWER`, `◆ MIXED`), green/red diffs for **Damage**, **Attack Speed**, **Est. DPS**, **Mining Speed**, **Armor**, **Toughness**, **KB Resistance**, **Durability**, and **Enchantment** gains/losses (with Smart Hotbar Match and optional Hold-Key mode).
- [NEW] **Potion Brewing Guide (`WORLD`)**: Interactive glassmorphic Brewing Guide panel beside the Brewing Stand UI with vanilla potion recipes, ingredient icons, live inventory ownership checks (`✓`), an `All`/`Have` filter, `+Time`/`+II`/invert badges, and a modifier legend.
- [NEW] **Furnace Helper (`WORLD`)**: Glassmorphic side panel in Furnaces, Blast Furnaces (`2×`), and Smokers (`2×`) displaying exact remaining stack smelt time (`mm:ss`), live smelt & burn bars, fuel needed (Coal, Blaze Rods, Kelp Blocks, Lava), and a **⚠ Fuel Waste Warning**.
- [NEW] **Enchant & Grindstone (`WORLD`)**: Scans the `5×2×5` bookshelf ring at Enchanting Tables (`0–15` bookshelves) and warns when torches/carpets/blocks obstruct gaps; previews exact vanilla XP orb/level returns and warns with **⚠ Treasure Enchant!** before stripping `Mending`, `Soul Speed`, `Swift Sneak`, `Wind Burst`, or `Frost Walker` at Grindstones.
- [NEW] **Chat History Search (`Chat` Module Setting)**: New `Chat Search (Ctrl+F)` setting inside the `Chat` module adding a `🔍 Search (Ctrl+F)` button above the chat bar to live-search chat history with highlighted matches and 1-click copy.
- [NEW] **Beacon Helper (`WORLD`)**: Displays Beacon Pyramid Tier (`0–4`), active radius (`±20m`–`±50m`), exact remaining mineral blocks for the next tier, sky-beam warnings, and active effects in the Beacon UI — plus glowing 3D Beacon coverage boundaries and `Inside`/`Outside` badges in the world.
- [NEW] **Redstone Inspector (`WORLD`)**: Clean crosshair HUD badge showing exact Redstone power (`0–15` with a 15-segment bar), Repeater delay/lock, Comparator mode, Note Block musical pitch (`F#3`–`F#5`) & instrument, Copper Bulbs/Pistons/Hoppers/Observers, and comparator levels for Composters, Cauldrons, Cakes, Beehives, Respawn Anchors, and more.
---

## Shortcuts & Controls
- Press `Right Shift` to open the **ClickGUI** menu.
- Use the **HUD Editor** button to drag and position your widgets on screen.
- Press `ESC` or click the close button to exit this changelog.
